Product Details

The SG5 is a lightweight wireless gaming mouse built for players who want fast, consistent tracking without being tied to a cable. It pairs a high-resolution optical sensor with a shell that is shaped to sit comfortably in the palm during long sessions, whether you play fast-paced shooters or slower strategy titles.

Dual mode connectivity lets you switch between a low-latency wireless link and a wired connection, so the mouse is ready when the battery is not. The included wireless charging dock keeps it topped up on your desk, and the dual function extension dock also helps extend the wireless signal range for a cleaner, more reliable connection.

Click feel matters as much as speed. RapidSnap Rebound Technology and dual rebound click spring flaps give each press a crisp, quick return, while TrueTap Response keeps input delay low. The relocated CPI button sits out of the way of your grip, so you do not change sensitivity by accident in the middle of a match.

Fine-tune everything with the Wireless M-Esport Editor: set your preferred CPI levels, adjust the six sensitivity steps, and save the profile that suits each game. The anti-slip grip tape on the sides keeps the mouse steady when your hands get warm.
